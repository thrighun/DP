As semiconductor technology keeps moving forward, undeterred by the many challenges ahead, defects in SEM images can significantly impact the performance of devices. SEM images often contain noise and artifacts that make defect detection challenging. Traditional methods rely on manual inspection or software that may not provide precise edge roughness quantification. The need for an automated, reliable, and user-friendly tool to extract meaningful roughness metrics from SEM images is crucial for advancing material analysis and quality control.\\

The objective of this project is to develop a web-based tool that processes SEM images, to classify the images into line and contact spaces images, and calculates critical dimensions (CD), line-edge roughness (LER) and line-width roughness (LWR) for line spaces and calculates average diameter and elliptically for contact spaces accurately. The tool also displays Power spectral density (PSD) curves for images.\\
